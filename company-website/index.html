Who We Are

About FinSpark

A proudly South African company building purpose-driven technology for financial inclusion, rehabilitation, and economic empowerment.

FinSpark was founded on a simple belief: access to financial education is a right, not a privilege. We design and build digital products that meet people where they are — in townships, in correctional facilities, in classrooms, and on the factory floor — and equip them with the tools to take control of their financial futures.

Our flagship platform brings gamified, culturally relevant financial education to communities across South Africa, with a special focus on supporting correctional reintegration programmes by reducing recidivism through skills-based rehabilitation.

"Umntu ngumntu ngabantu" A person is a person through others — the isiXhosa proverb that guides everything we build.

Innovation

We combine behavioural science, gamification, and culturally grounded storytelling to make financial education stick.

Empowerment

Every module is designed to convert knowledge into action — from opening a savings account to reading a JSE share price.

Impact

We measure success not in downloads, but in net worth growth, debt reduction, and second chances created.

The Platform

Meet FinSpark

FinSpark is a fully gamified financial literacy simulator built for the South African market. Through interactive lessons, real-world JSE market simulations, community stokvel mechanics, and a built-in anti-scam academy, users learn to budget, save, invest, and protect themselves — all while levelling up, earning XP, and unlocking verified certificates.

SAPS SynchroLearn: built in partnership with correctional reintegration programmes, our institutional portal gives facilitators a dedicated dashboard to manage offender cases, track quiz performance, and generate parole-ready progress reports.
  • 17 personalised learning paths across 5 audience groups
  • Live JSE-style market simulator with real trading mechanics
  • Stokvel community savings simulator with payout voting
  • SHA-256 verified certificates with expiring QR codes
  • SAPS SynchroLearn institutional portal with parole reporting
  • Available in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho & Afrikaans

Key Features

Everything You Need to Build Real Financial Skills

FinSpark combines gamification, real-world simulation, and verifiable credentials into one premium experience.

Gamified Learning Paths

Earn XP, unlock badges, and level up through 17 mindset-based journeys covering budgeting, saving, investing, and entrepreneurship.

JSE Market Simulator

Practice buying and selling JSE-style assets with live price charts, moving-average signals, and full trade history — zero financial risk.

Verified Certificates

Every completed pathway generates a tamper-proof, SHA-256-secured certificate with a time-limited QR code for instant verification.

SAPS SynchroLearn Portal

A dedicated facilitator dashboard for offender case management, quiz scoring, and exportable parole progress reports.

5-Language Support

Fully localised in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, and Afrikaans — financial education in the language that feels like home.

Stokvel Simulator

Experience South Africa's iconic rotating savings model with community payout voting and group accountability.

Anti-Scam Academy

20 real-world phishing and scam scenarios across 6 categories train users to spot fraud before it costs them.

Offline-First PWA

Installable on any device and fully functional without an internet connection — built for low-data environments.

Impact & Mission

Crime Prevention Through Financial Literacy

Financial desperation is one of the most consistent precursors to reoffending. FinSpark exists to interrupt that cycle. By equipping incarcerated and at-risk South Africans with practical money skills — and giving correctional facilitators the tools to track and verify progress — FinSpark turns rehabilitation into a measurable, repeatable process.

Every certificate earned is more than a credential — it's evidence of changed thinking, presented to parole boards, employers, and communities as proof that a person is ready to build, not break.
